# Code of conduct

This project adopts [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
This condensed guide adds the project's reporting contact; consult the full
Covenant for its standards and enforcement guidance.

Welcome participants across backgrounds and identities. Communicate respectfully,
accept constructive feedback, credit contributions, and take responsibility
for harm. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwelcome sexual
attention, impersonation, and exposing private information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
[miserpersonal@gmail.com](mailto:miserpersonal@gmail.com). Include relevant
context and evidence. The maintainer investigates reports and handles personal
information confidentially, sharing only what is needed for the investigation
and response.

Depending on severity and repetition, enforcement may include a private warning,
restricted participation, suspension, or removal. Serious incidents may skip
earlier steps. The aim is to address harm and protect participation.
